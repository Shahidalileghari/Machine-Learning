# Problem Statement

## Problem
What practical question will the model help investigate?

## Prediction target
Define the target, units/classes, and prediction time.

## Intended use
Who might use the result, and for what low-risk purpose?

## Non-goals
What decisions should not be made from this model?

## Success criteria
Choose metrics and explain the cost of false positives, false negatives, or numeric error.

## Data assumptions
What population and time period do the data represent? What may be missing?

## Risks and limitations
List leakage risks, sampling bias, fairness concerns, and failure modes.
